== Why CNML is the flagship

The OIML Certificat Numérique de Métrologie Légale (CNML) project is
the Mode 3 flagship because it demonstrates Confium at the highest-stakes
end of the spectrum:

* International treaty organization (OIML, 60+ member states)
* Nation-state adversary (both cyber and political)
* Decades-long archival (instruments last 10-20 years)
* Sovereignty sensitivity (no single nation trusted)
* Globally distributed directors (async signing required)
* Annual ceremony cadence (root operations only)

== Five-tier hierarchy

image::../images/cnml-tier-hierarchy.svg[CNML tier hierarchy]

|===
|Tier |Signing |Encryption |Threshold |Ceremony
|BIML |FROST-Ed25519 + ML-DSA-65 composite |ML-KEM-768 threshold |5-of-7 directors |Annual sync + emergency async
|IA |FROST-P256 |ElGamal-P256 threshold |2-of-3 officers |Async
|TL |ECDSA-P256 single-party |ECIES-P256 single-party |1-of-1 |Async
|Mfr-Model |ECDSA-P256 single-party |ECIES-P256 single-party |1-of-1 |Async
|Mfr-Instance |ECDSA-P256 single-party |n/a |1-of-1 |Async
|===

== Delegated signing at manufacturer tier

The IA's Manufacturer Model Certificate is a scoped delegation:
manufacturer can issue instance certs for instruments of a specific
model only.

== Async signing workflow

image::../images/async-session-lifecycle.svg[Async lifecycle]

1. Director enters passphrase → YubiKey decrypts wrapping key → share unwrapped
2. App generates Round 1 commitment (FROST nonce)
3. Commitment signed by YubiKey identity key
4. Commitment uploaded to coordinator
5. Director walks away (session stays unlocked ~4 hours)
6. Coordinator broadcasts aggregated commitments when T received
7. App generates Round 2 share
8. Share signed by YubiKey, uploaded
9. Coordinator aggregates T shares → signature
10. Full transcript audit-logged
